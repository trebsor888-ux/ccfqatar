What is a Dgroup?

A Dgroup, short for discipleship group, is a small group of people who meet regularly, share their lives with each other, study the Bible together, and hold one another accountable — all in the pursuit of becoming more Christ-like.

Why should you join a Dgroup?

C

Community

It fosters fellowship and is a safe space to share life with each other.

"Bear one another's burdens, and thereby fulfill the law of Christ." — Galatians 6:2

C

Care

It is a spiritual family where people support, encourage, and pray for each other.

"Let us consider how to stimulate one another to love and good deeds... encouraging one another." — Hebrews 10:24-25

C

Character

It helps build character through mutual accountability and building each other up.

"Iron sharpens iron, so one man sharpens another." — Proverbs 27:17

C

Collaboration

It is a place where serving God can be done shoulder to shoulder.

"As each one has received a gift, employ it in serving one another." — 1 Peter 4:10
